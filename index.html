DealSparkBot

DealSparkBot is the web crawler of DealSpark, a business lead tool that is still in development. It reads public company websites so that business contact details stay correct. This page says what it reads, how it behaves and how to stop it.

What it reads

  • Your home page and up to four contact, about or team pages: never more than 5 pages per site.
  • Business details only: company name, address, business phone numbers and shared mailboxes such as info@ or sales@.
  • It does not save names of people at present.
  • Never: pages behind a login, forms, captchas, social network profiles, or guessed email addresses.

How it behaves

  • It obeys your robots.txt before every page, including after redirects.
  • It waits at least 2 seconds between requests to your site, or longer if your robots.txt sets a Crawl-delay.
  • It stops visiting your site if it gets “403 Forbidden” and slows down on “429 Too Many Requests”.
  • It identifies itself as DealSparkBot with a link to this page.

How to stop it

Add these lines to your robots.txt and the bot will not read your site:

User-agent: DealSparkBot
Disallow: /
